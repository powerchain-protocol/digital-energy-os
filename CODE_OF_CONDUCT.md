# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We, as members, contributors, and maintainers of the PowerChain community, pledge to make participation in our project and community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

---

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and considerate of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Assuming good intent while communicating clearly and professionally.
- Showing empathy toward other community members.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the project.
- Helping new contributors learn and participate.
- Respecting community decisions even when disagreements occur.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or abusive conduct.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwelcome advances.
- Deliberate intimidation, stalking, or following.
- Disruptive or hostile behavior during discussions or events.
- Spam, malicious code, or intentionally harmful contributions.
- Any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional environment.

---

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other contributions that are inconsistent with this Code of Conduct.

When appropriate, maintainers will communicate the reasons for moderation decisions.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all PowerChain community spaces, including but not limited to:

- GitHub repositories
- GitHub Issues
- GitHub Discussions
- Pull Requests
- Documentation
- Community chat platforms
- Conferences and meetups organized by the project
- Any public space where an individual is officially representing PowerChain

Examples of representing the project include:

- Using an official project email address.
- Posting from an official PowerChain social media account.
- Acting as a maintainer, moderator, or representative at community events.

---

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it privately to the project maintainers.

When submitting a report, include as much relevant information as possible, such as:

- A description of the incident
- Links or screenshots (if available)
- Date and time
- Individuals involved
- Any additional context that may help investigate

Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with respect for the privacy and safety of everyone involved.

---

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers will evaluate each incident fairly and consistently.

Possible actions include:

### 1. Correction

Used for minor or first-time violations.

- A private reminder explaining why the behavior was inappropriate.
- Guidance on how to avoid similar behavior in the future.

### 2. Warning

Used for repeated or more serious violations.

- A formal warning.
- Temporary restrictions on participation if necessary.

### 3. Temporary Ban

Used for significant misconduct.

Examples include:

- Harassment
- Personal attacks
- Repeated disruptive behavior
- Refusal to comply with moderator requests

Duration will depend on the severity of the incident.

### 4. Permanent Ban

Reserved for severe or repeated violations, including:

- Threats of violence
- Hate speech
- Sustained harassment
- Malicious attacks against contributors
- Intentional distribution of malware or harmful code
- Repeated violations after prior enforcement actions
