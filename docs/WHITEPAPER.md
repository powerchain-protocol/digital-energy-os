# PowerChain Digital Energy Infrastructure — Whitepaper

**Canonical version: 1.0.0**

PowerChain is an AI-native operating and settlement platform for renewable infrastructure, tokenized real-world assets, connected energy systems and governed economic agents.

## Operating doctrine

```text
OBSERVE
   ↓
UNDERSTAND
   ↓
ANALYZE
   ↓
RECOMMEND
   ↓
PREPARE
   ↓
VERIFY
   ↓
APPROVE
   ↓
EXECUTE
   ↓
AUDIT
```

Physical systems remain authoritative for physical energy. The Energy Ledger establishes verified energy state; token representation, blockchain inclusion and financial settlement remain separate domains.

## Platform layers

- Company OS — organizations, participants, ERP, CMR, policy and operating context.
- Energy OS — EMS, smart metering, IoT/DePIN devices, Local Energy and evidence.
- RWA OS — verified positions, tokenization, CCT and provenance.
- Copilot — bounded AI analysis, evidence, recommendations and action intents.
- Control Plane — identity, authorization, context, policy, approvals and audit.
- Execution Gateways — Solana, Sui and external APIs with independent wallet/system authority.
- Treasury & Revenue — precise economic accounting, allocations, reconciliation and close.

## Copilot credits

The Copilot credit system is PWRC-backed but does not transfer PWRC on-chain per message. Base usage is $0.020 / 10,000 PWRC at the canonical $0.000002 reference. Authenticated real-data usage is $0.015 / 7,500 PWRC. EUR and SOL equivalents are sourced display conversions.

## Energy Network

Consumers, prosumers, retailers, renewable generators, utilities, communities, grid operators and partners participate through a tenant-aware Energy Network. Local Energy supports BUY, SELL and RENT with atomic integer-Wh reservations.

## Token and network integration

PowerChain supports SPL Token, Token-2022, Metaplex, Pyth, Jupiter, Helius/Helium and Sui integration boundaries. External APIs and on-chain programs are evidence/execution dependencies, not sources of unrestricted application authority.
