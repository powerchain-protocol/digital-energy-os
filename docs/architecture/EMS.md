# PowerChain EMS v1.0.0

`apps/ems/` is the first-class Energy Management System runtime. It proxies canonical platform resources and health/readiness without creating duplicate domain state.

Operator types: company, partnership, electricity retailer, energy company, utility, grid operator, community.

Modules: monitoring, metering, connected devices, local energy, billing, settlement, forecasting, renewables, rewards and treasury.
